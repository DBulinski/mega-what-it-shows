How to tell them apart

  • parser: day and month swap (7 April becomes 4 July). The tenant writes the day first, and the report did not come through booking-v3’s form.
  • hint: the same swap, but through booking-v3’s form. The form prints MM/DD/YYYY under the date field; people type what it says and the form reads it day first. Fixing the parser does not touch this: the hint is wrong.
  • shift: no swap. The date lands one day early (Tuesday 12 May shows as Monday 11 May), or depends on where the reader is (Lisbon gets Tuesday, Toronto gets Monday). A date with no time is stored as midnight UTC and shown in the viewer’s time zone. The desk already lists these reports in desk-notes.json; this page counts them as their own problem.

Intake’s parser / hint tag is a guess from the reporter’s browser. It disagrees with the tenant on many reports, so the table above places reports by tenant and form, never by tag.

Why they were merged

Decision 0001 merges reports with the same subject line. All three problems produce the same subjects (“Wrong day on the invite”, “Date is wrong”), so subject matching put them in one ticket. Merging by cause, as the triage explorer already does, keeps them apart.
